Strategic Tips for Kiel Week:

The Parade (Windjammer-Parade): The ships move from the inner harbor out toward the sea. 
For the best view without paying for a boat, walk along the Kiellinie (the harbor promenade).

The "Bus 12/13" Note: these buses go from Kiel Hbf toward "An der Schanze" or "Brauner Berg." 
These spots are further down the fjord and offer a great view of the ships with slightly fewer crowds than the city center.

Transport: Your Deutschlandticket or Schleswig-Holstein-Ticket covers the train and the buses in Kiel.

Crowd Warning: Expect "festival conditions." Use the bathroom when you see one, and keep your valuables secure.

Hamburg Sunset: In late June, the sun sets very late (around 9:50 PM). 
Arriving at Stintfang by 20:30 gives you the perfect "Blue Hour" light as the harbor lights turn on.

Pro Tip for Stintfang: If the main hill is too crowded, 
there is a small "secret" terrace slightly lower down near 
the entrance to the U-Bahn station that still has a great view of the Rickmer Rickmers ship!
