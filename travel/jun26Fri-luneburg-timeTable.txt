Essential Tips for this Trip:

Getting to Isemarkt: Take the U3 (Yellow line) to station Eppendorfer Baum.
The market starts right outside the exit and stretches for nearly 1km under the railway bridge.

The "Must-Buy" at Isemarkt: Look for Franzbrötchen (Hamburg’s signature cinnamon pastry).
They are perfect to carry to Lüneburg for a mid-afternoon snack.

Lüneburg’s "Leaning" Houses: Head to the Senktungsgebiet (subsidence area).
Because of centuries of salt mining, the ground shifted, making the brick houses look like
they are melting or leaning at wild angles.
