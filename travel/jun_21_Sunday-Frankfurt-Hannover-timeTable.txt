Neues Rathaus (New City Hall): Do not miss the four city models in the main hall. They show Hannover in 1689, 1939, 1945 (almost totally flat), and today. It helps you understand why the city looks the way it does.

Aegidienkirche: This is a powerful site. The church was left as a ruin after WWII. Look for the Peace Bell (a gift from Hiroshima) inside.

Marktkirche & Altes Rathaus: These are right across from each other. The Altes Rathaus (Old Town Hall) is one of the most beautiful examples of North German Brick Gothic architecture.

The Bread Quest (Jochen Gaues)

Jochen Gaues is known for his "Ochsenbrot".

Sunday Tip: Most of his local boutique outlets are closed on Sundays.
However, the Edeka in Hannover Hbf (open 24/7) often has a specialty bread counter that carries artisan sourdoughs.
check the Edeka Wucherpfennig inside the Hauptbahnhof (Basement level).
Ask for "Krustenbrot" (Crusty bread) to get that famous Gaues-style thick, dark crust.

Elevator Wait: On a Sunday in June, the wait for the New Town Hall dome elevator
can be 20–30 minutes. Since you have a half-day, go there first (around 14:00) to secure your spot.
