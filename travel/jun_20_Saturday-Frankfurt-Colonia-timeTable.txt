The "Old Building" Walking Route (14:15 – 15:30)

Since you want to see old buildings, follow this path from the Cathedral:
Town Hall (Rathaus): A mix of Gothic and Renaissance styles. Look for the "Loggia."
Farina House: Directly opposite the Town Hall.
Great St. Martin: The massive church with the four corner towers—it dominates the Old Town skyline.
Fish Market (Fischmarkt): Located on the riverbank, featuring the iconic colorful narrow houses.

Snack Strategy
--
Since you want to keep lunch "easy":
The Station: Cologne Hbf has excellent bakeries (Ditsch, Kamps) for pretzels or sandwiches you can eat while walking.
Alter Markt: On Saturdays, there are often high-quality food stalls selling local snacks.
