Depart Berlin Hbf	08:41 AM	Take the RE7 (Direction: Dessau Hbf).
Arrive Dessau Hbf	10:19 AM	The station is the starting point for the "Bauhaus Line."

10:30 The Bauhaus Building
A 10-minute walk from the station. This is the icon of modern architecture.
Activity: Wander the grounds or take a guided tour of the famous glass-curtain wall building designed by Walter Gropius.
The Bauhaus Museum: Located right in the city center (near the station), this new museum houses the second-largest collection of Bauhaus-related items in the world.

12:30 The Masters' Houses (Meisterhäuser) (12:30 – 14:00)
 Walk 10 minutes through a pine-tree park to reach the houses where famous artists like Kandinsky and Klee lived.
 The Vibe: Minimalist white cubes tucked into the forest. It’s incredibly photogenic in the July sun.

14:00 Lunch at the Elbe: Kornhaus Take Bus 10 or walk/bike north to the Kornhaus.
 The Experience: A Bauhaus-style restaurant sitting right on the banks of the Elbe River.
 The Move: Have lunch on the terrace with a view of the river. It’s a classic Dessau summer tradition.

Wörlitz Garden Kingdom (Optional / Late Afternoon)
 If you still have energy, hop on the "Dessau-Wörlitzer Eisenbahn" 
 (a small heritage train, check if D-Ticket is accepted on the day or pay a small fee) to the Wörlitz Park.

Why: It’s a UNESCO World Heritage site and one of the first English-style landscape gardens in Europe. You can take a gondola ride on the canals—perfect for a warm July evening.

Bicycle Rental: Dessau is extremely flat and bike-friendly. 
You can rent a bike at the Dessau Hbf. In July, cycling between the Bauhaus sites and the river is much nicer than taking the bus.
